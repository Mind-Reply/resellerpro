# ResellerPro — Competitive Operating Register

## Product thesis

ResellerPro should compete above transaction processing by making the entire domain/hosting lifecycle observable, recoverable and economically legible.

### Four proof pillars

- **Every transaction is provable** — ledger + reconciliation + evidence.
- **Every decision is informed** — margin, pricing, renewal and portfolio signals.
- **Every workflow is connected** — domain, hosting, commerce, deployment, support and operations.
- **Every customer is guided** — contextual recommendations with explicit control boundaries.

## Differentiation backlog

### Critical
- Transaction/evidence ledger
- Reconciliation and recovery states
- Domain → hosting → deployment lifecycle visibility
- Margin transparency
- Owner approval boundary for purchases and recurring commitments

### High
- One-dashboard operating view
- Renewal intelligence
- Regional storefront model: EU/CEE first, then UK, MENA, Americas
- Intent-signal layer
- Retrieval/mention/sentiment/third-party validation tracking

### Normal
- Competitive comparison content
- Agency beachhead tooling
- Case-study measurement framework
- SEO/partner attribution

## Campaign guardrail

The proposed “Zero Failed Domains” campaign is **not publishable as a factual claim yet**. Only publish a failure/refund statistic when the ledger contains a defined population, period, denominator, methodology and independently verifiable evidence. Never invent or borrow an incumbent's refund rate to imply superiority.

## Beachhead measurement

For an agency customer, measure before/after:

- managed domains
- monthly admin hours
- renewal completion rate
- provisioning failure rate
- gross margin by TLD/service
- support escalations
- time to resolve failures
- portfolio expansion/revenue

The strongest competitive claim should come from measured customer outcomes, not slogans.

## Experience principles

1. **Value visible immediately.** Pricing and next action should never be hidden.
2. **State before decoration.** Customers see what is live, pending, failing or renewing.
3. **One account, one truth.** Domains, services, projects and billing share one lifecycle model.
4. **Fast path first.** Search → buy → provision should be the shortest journey.
5. **Trust through evidence.** Important changes show status, timestamps and recoverability.
6. **Competitive without copying.** Learn from registrar/hosting leaders but maintain a distinct ResellerPro identity.

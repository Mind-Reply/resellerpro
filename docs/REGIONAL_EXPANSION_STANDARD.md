# ResellerPro — Regional Expansion Standard

Status: EXECUTION STANDARD

## Expansion order

ResellerPro launches and proves two native-grade foundations first:
1. Bulgaria
2. United Kingdom

Only after the Bulgaria + UK foundations are completely settled, production-verified and operationally stable does the platform progress to wider European expansion and then evidence-led research for selected Asia and South America/Latin America markets.

This is a sequencing rule, not a limitation of the platform architecture.

## Native-market principle

A regional market is not considered native because the interface has been translated.

Each market pack must align:
- Language and terminology
- Currency and monetary formatting
- Date, time and address conventions
- Domain/TLD catalogue
- Registrar and registry requirements
- Hosting/service availability
- Tax and legal presentation
- Payment methods
- Pricing presentation
- Customer support language and workflows
- SEO and acquisition content
- Consent and policy presentation
- Local operational constraints
- Evidence and verification requirements

The underlying ResellerPro domain/provider operating model remains shared.

## Phase 1 — Bulgaria

Required native foundation:
- Bulgarian (bg-BG)
- English (en-GB/international)
- BGN and EUR presentation
- .bg and .бг domain support where operationally available
- EU consumer, privacy and tax presentation
- Bulgarian customer journey and support language
- Bulgarian domain/registrar lifecycle rules
- Localized checkout, invoices and transactional communications
- Evidence for actual registry/provider capability before any capability is shown as available

## Phase 1 — United Kingdom

Required native foundation:
- British English (en-GB)
- GBP and applicable international pricing
- .uk and .co.uk domain support where operationally available
- UK-specific domain lifecycle terminology
- UK billing, checkout and tax/legal presentation
- UK addresses, dates, currency and support conventions
- UK-localized acquisition and SEO surfaces
- Evidence for actual registry/provider capability before any capability is shown as available

## Phase 2 — Europe

After BG + UK are production-settled, expand through configurable regional packs rather than application forks.

Candidate research sequence:
- DACH
- France
- Italy
- Spain
- Netherlands
- Poland
- Nordics
- Balkans / Central and Eastern Europe

Each market must pass the same evidence gate before launch.

## Phase 3 — Asia

Asia is a research portfolio, not a single localization.

Markets are selected individually using:
- Demand
- Domain-market size and growth
- Competitive structure
- Registry/provider access
- Localization depth
- Payment readiness
- Regulatory complexity
- Hosting/provider economics
- Customer acquisition economics
- Operational support requirements

No Asia market is treated as live or launch-ready solely because a locale exists in configuration.

## Phase 4 — South America / Latin America

LATAM is evaluated as individual country/market packs.

Selection criteria are the same as Asia, with additional attention to:
- Local domain ecosystems
- Spanish/Portuguese localization
- Local payment rails
- Tax/invoicing requirements
- Registry relationships
- Regional hosting economics
- Cross-border operational constraints

## Evidence gate

Every regional capability progresses through:
RESEARCH → REQUIREMENTS → APPLICATION READY → SUBMITTED → ACCREDITED → TECHNICAL TEST → LIVE → VERIFIED

Legal submissions, contracts, accreditation, registry relationships and payment-provider enablement require the appropriate owner approval.

A configured locale, marketing page or database record is not evidence of production capability.

## Platform architecture

Regional packs must remain configuration and policy layers above the shared platform core:

Identity → Workspace → Domain → Provider → Service → Execution → Deployment → Runtime → Evidence

Regionalization must never create parallel identity, billing, provider, execution, deployment or evidence systems.

## Release rule

The public product may expose only capabilities supported by current evidence.
- No fabricated market availability.
- No simulated registry access.
- No invented pricing.
- No unverified provider status.
- No unverified accreditation.
- No market claim presented as operational fact.

## Definition of done for BG + UK foundation

Both markets are considered settled only when:
- Native language surfaces are implemented and reviewed
- Domain catalogue/capability is evidence-backed
- Provider/registrar capability is evidence-backed
- Hosting/service catalogue is evidence-backed
- Checkout and billing flows are regionally correct
- Legal/tax presentation is implemented for the intended operating scope
- Customer communications are localized
- SEO/acquisition surfaces are localized
- Mobile flows are production-ready
- Runtime health is verified
- Critical journeys have evidence
- Regional limitations are explicit
- No synthetic metrics or availability claims remain

Only then should the next regional expansion gate open.

# ResellerPro Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 2026
Product: ResellerPro

This document is a product privacy baseline. The final legal controller identity, statutory address, contact details, retention periods and processor schedule must match the actual production entity and processing configuration before public launch.

## Information we may process

Depending on the service, ResellerPro may process account and contact information, authentication identifiers, domain-registration information, billing and order records, support communications, DNS/site configuration, security and audit events, device/network metadata, and information voluntarily submitted by users.

## Purposes and legal bases

Processing may be required to provide and secure accounts and services, process orders and payments, satisfy domain-registry and legal requirements, detect fraud and abuse, provide support, maintain audit and reconciliation records, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

Where GDPR applies, the applicable legal basis depends on the particular processing activity and may include contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent.

## Providers and subprocessors

ResellerPro may use payment processors, registrars, hosting providers, database providers, email providers, security services and model/API providers. Their roles are governed by applicable service arrangements and legal terms.

## Model/API processing

Where external model services are enabled, relevant inputs may be transmitted to the selected provider according to the configured integration and applicable provider terms. Users should not submit secrets or unnecessary personal data to external model services.

## Retention and international transfers

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, contractual service, security/audit need, legal obligation, dispute resolution or legitimate operational requirement, subject to applicable law.

Some providers may process information outside the user's country. Where required, appropriate safeguards and transfer mechanisms will be used.

## Security

ResellerPro is designed around least privilege, fail-closed mutations, audit evidence, controlled provider adapters, externalized secrets and explicit authorization for high-impact operations. No security measure guarantees absolute security.

## Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Requests may require identity verification.

## Cookies

The service may use essential cookies and similar mechanisms required for authentication, security and operation. Optional analytics or marketing technologies should be enabled only with applicable consent and disclosure controls.

## Changes

This notice may be updated when processing, providers, law or service architecture changes.

## Contact

The production notice must identify the actual legal controller and statutory contact details applicable to the production service.

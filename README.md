# ResellerPro

**Domains · Hosting · Commerce · Projects · Support · Operations**

ResellerPro is the Mind-Reply commerce and infrastructure platform: a single operating experience for discovering domains, launching hosting, managing projects, handling billing, and continuously operating digital assets.

## Astra 5.6 direction

ResellerPro is being built to compete with established domain/hosting leaders through a stronger operating model rather than a clone of their interfaces:

- transparent pricing and renewal visibility
- domain-to-deployment lifecycle in one workspace
- fast checkout and provisioning
- proactive health and renewal intelligence
- operational evidence and recovery paths
- customer and owner controls with safe automation
- regional storefronts backed by one canonical platform
- global-ready service architecture

### Core lifecycle

`Discover → Compare → Configure → Checkout → Provision → Verify → Operate → Renew → Expand`

### Initial market lanes

Global · EU/CEE · UK · MENA · Americas

See [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md) for the canonical product and infrastructure design.

## Build rule

A feature is presented as live only after its underlying customer journey has been tested end-to-end. Billing, provisioning, destructive operations, and customer communications require explicit control and recovery handling.

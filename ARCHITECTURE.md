# ResellerPro — Astra 5.6 Competitive Architecture

## Position

ResellerPro is the commerce and operations platform for domains, hosting, projects, billing, support, and managed digital infrastructure.

The target is not to imitate incumbent registrars feature-for-feature. The target is a cleaner, faster, more transparent operating experience with automation, evidence, and service continuity built into the product.

## Product pillars

1. **Domains** — search, registration, renewal, transfer, DNS and portfolio management.
2. **Hosting** — deploy, configure, monitor and manage websites and applications.
3. **Commerce** — plans, checkout, invoices, renewals, credits and service lifecycle.
4. **Projects** — connect domains, hosting, deployments and customer work into one workspace.
5. **Support** — structured tickets, status, knowledge and escalation.
6. **Operations** — health, incidents, releases, backups, audit evidence and recovery.
7. **Intelligence** — recommendations, portfolio health, renewal risk, configuration checks and opportunity signals.

## Competitive wedge

Compete on **clarity + automation + operational visibility**, not only price.

- Simple pricing and renewal visibility.
- One dashboard from domain to deployed site.
- Proactive health and renewal warnings.
- Safe automation with explicit owner/customer approval boundaries.
- Evidence attached to important changes.
- Regional storefronts backed by one canonical operating core.
- Global-ready architecture without duplicating customer truth.

## Canonical journey

`Discover → Compare → Configure → Checkout → Provision → Verify → Operate → Renew → Expand`

Every stage must have a recoverable state and an auditable event.

## Regional/global model

Use one canonical product and data model. Localize presentation, currency, language, tax handling, payment rails, legal copy, support hours, and eligible products by market. Never create separate operational sources of truth per region.

Initial market lanes:
- Global
- EU / CEE
- UK
- MENA
- Americas

## Visual direction

Premium infrastructure control center: high information density without clutter, strong typography, clear status states, fast comparison, and obvious primary actions. The customer should understand cost, state, next action, and risk without searching through menus.

## Build gates

- No production commerce claim without a working underlying flow.
- No billing integration without reconciliation and failure handling.
- No automated destructive action without an approval boundary.
- No public feature marketed as live until end-to-end tested.
- Every provisioning path requires verification and recovery handling.
